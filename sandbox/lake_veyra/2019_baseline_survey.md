# Lake Veyra Water Quality Program — 2019 Baseline Survey

Prepared by the Veyra Watershed Council, November 2019.

## Scope
The Council established 14 sampling stations across the lake: 8 in the north basin and 6 in the south basin. Samples were collected monthly from April through October.

## Findings
- Mean total phosphorus (TP) in the north basin was 38 µg/L during the sampling season.
- Mean TP in the south basin was 21 µg/L.
- Average Secchi depth (water clarity) across all stations was 2.1 m.
- Harmful algal blooms were recorded on 41 days in 2019, almost all in the north basin.

## Interpretation
The north basin is eutrophic. The difference between basins points to an external nutrient source entering the north end of the lake, where Tamsin Creek and two smaller drainage ditches discharge.

## Recommendation
Quantify nutrient loads from tributaries before selecting interventions.
