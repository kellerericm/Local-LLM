VEYRA WATERSHED COUNCIL
Tributary Loading Study and Agricultural Runoff Report
March 2021

Summary
Using flow-weighted sampling at tributary mouths during 2020, we estimate that Tamsin Creek contributes about 55% of the external phosphorus load to Lake Veyra. Most of that load arrives during spring snowmelt and storms that follow fertilizer application on row-crop fields upstream.

Other sources
Shoreline septic systems: approximately 15% of the external load.
Urban stormwater from the town of Carrow: approximately 10%.
Remaining sources (atmospheric deposition, minor ditches): approximately 20%.

Actions taken in 2021
The Council partnered with farmers to install vegetated buffer strips along Tamsin Creek. By the end of 2021, buffer strips were in place on 12 farms, covering 9.4 km of streambank.
A cost-share program for septic inspections was opened to 300 shoreline properties.

Limitations
Loads were estimated from a single year of data (2020), which had above-average spring rainfall.
