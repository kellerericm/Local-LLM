# 2022 Monitoring Update

Veyra Watershed Council — January 2023

## Network
A 15th sampling station was added at the mouth of Tamsin Creek in 2022.

## Results
| Measure | 2019 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|
| North basin mean TP (µg/L) | 38 | 29 |
| South basin mean TP (µg/L) | 21 | 20 |
| Algal bloom days | 41 | 27 |
| Mean Secchi depth (m) | 2.1 | 2.3 |

## Notes
Improvement in the north basin began one season after buffer strips were installed. Because 2022 was a dry year, part of the decline may reflect lower runoff rather than the interventions alone.
