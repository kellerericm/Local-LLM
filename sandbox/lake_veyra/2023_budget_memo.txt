MEMO — Program Budget
To: Council Board
From: Finance Committee
Date: October 2023

The Lake Veyra Water Quality Program's 2023 budget was $2.4 million, funded mainly by a state clean-water grant.

The state grant will be reduced, so the program budget will be cut by 15% in 2024. To keep all 15 sampling stations running, the committee recommends replacing two paid technician positions with a trained volunteer sampling corps.

The septic cost-share program will be paused in 2024. The buffer strip maintenance fund is protected.
