# Independent Review of Tributary Load Estimates

Harlow & Finch Environmental Consulting — August 2023

We reviewed the Council's 2021 tributary loading study. Using three years of data (2020–2022) and a sediment core analysis, we estimate that Tamsin Creek contributes roughly 30% of the external phosphorus load, not the 55% reported in 2021.

We believe the 2021 figure is too high because it relied on a single unusually wet year. Our analysis attributes a larger share to internal loading: phosphorus released from north basin sediments during late-summer low-oxygen periods.

If internal loading is significant, watershed measures alone may not bring north basin TP below 25 µg/L. We recommend evaluating in-lake treatment, such as aeration or alum, alongside continued buffer strip work.
