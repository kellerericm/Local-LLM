# 2024 Monitoring Update

Veyra Watershed Council — December 2024

## Results
- North basin mean TP: 26 µg/L (29 in 2022).
- South basin mean TP: 20 µg/L.
- Algal bloom days: 22.
- Mean Secchi depth: 2.6 m.

## Observations
Late-summer TP spikes were recorded at the two deepest north basin stations in August, when bottom-water oxygen was low. This is consistent with internal loading from sediments.

Volunteer samplers collected 61% of all samples in 2024, with quality-control results comparable to staff samples.
